### Supabase
- Free: 500 MB database, 1 GB storage, 50 MB max upload, pauses after 1 week idle, 2 active free projects, no automatic backups.
- Pro: from $25 a month, $10 compute credit (Micro: 1 GB RAM, shared CPU, 60 direct connections), 8 GB disk, 100 GB storage, 250 GB egress then $0.09/GB, 7 days of daily backups, spend cap on by default.
- Queues: built on pgmq; exactly-once delivery within a visibility window; archive supported; dead-letter queue not documented. `pgmq_public` exposes send, send_batch, read, pop, archive, delete.
- Upstream pgmq: `read`, `read_with_poll`, `set_vt`, `archive`, `delete`, `pop` (at-most-once), FIFO grouped reads. Message fields include `read_ct` and `vt`. No built-in dead-letter queue.
- Regions: 17 specific AWS regions. "General" groups: Americas → Virginia, Europe → Frankfurt (may also place in London or Zurich), APAC → Singapore.

- Prices: Starter 0.5 CPU / 512 MB $7 a month; Standard 1 CPU / 2 GB $25 a month. Hobby workspace free (25 services); Pro workspace $25 a month flat. (First found via search; confirmed from the official pricing page in Pass 3.)

### Vercel

- **Resend:**

  - Customer data deleted within 90 days of account termination. 14 days' notice for subprocessor changes.
  - Acceptable use: bounce rate below 4%, complaints below 0.08%. No free-plan commercial restriction found.
- **AWS SES:** $0.10 per 1,000 emails; data residency not stated on the pricing page.
- **Cloudflare Turnstile:** free plan, unlimited challenges, 20 widgets; described as for "most production applications".
