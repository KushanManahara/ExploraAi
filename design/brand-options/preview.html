ExploraAi logo options

Three directions built from the design system: Familjen Grotesk 500, navy ink, paper, and the single electric-blue accent. Each shows the wordmark on paper and night, the app icon at 112, 64, 32 and 16 px, and a small wordmark.

Option A

Tonal wordmark

“Explora” in ink, “Ai” at 40%, and the accent dot as a full stop. It follows the design system’s wordmark pattern most closely, and the icon is a lowercase “e” plus the dot, like the existing km● icon.

Trade-off: Safest fit with the design system; quiet and readable. Least distinctive.

Option B

Focus lens

A ring with an accent dot off-centre: a lens that has found one exact point, the way an answer points to one page. The wordmark is a single tone.

Trade-off: Most ownable symbol; the icon works without text. The ring may read as a plain “o” at very small sizes.

Option C

Citation brackets

Brackets around an accent dot, like an in-text citation [●]. The wordmark pairs the grotesk “Explora” with a mono “AI” tag, reusing the design system’s tag style.

Trade-off: Speaks directly to the product’s core idea (answers with sources). The tag adds one more element to keep crisp at small sizes.
