Brand mark: two-tone wordmark (first name ink, surname muted, accent dot as full stop) or the "km●" monogram tile used for the app icon.
```jsx
<BrandMark />
<BrandMark variant="monogram" size={20} />
```
- Wordmark inherits `color`, so it flips automatically on night sections.
- The accent dot is the period that ends every headline — never swap it for a square or icon.
- Monogram is always navy tile + paper letters; don't recolor it.
