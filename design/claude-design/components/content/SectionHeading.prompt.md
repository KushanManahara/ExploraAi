Eyebrow + tight grotesk headline with a two-tone emphasis tail (same face, ink at 40%) + optional lead. The signature heading of every section.
```jsx
<SectionHeading eyebrow="Expertise" title={<>What I <Em>build.</Em></>}
  lead="Most projects touch three or four of these at once." />
```
- Exactly one `<Em>` phrase per headline, usually the last 1–3 words, often ending with a period.- `<Em tone="accent">` only for a single hero moment; never italics or a second typeface.
